# Pulse project map — Brief

*Internal Pulse. Design session with Dave, Oct 5, 2026. Gate verdict: GO (Ideas & Opportunities, "Pulse project map (internal Pulse)").*

## Business objective
Stop the team tripping over work that isn't getting done when it needs to. Projects are getting lost in the weeds of their own tasks. Nobody can see at a glance whether a project is moving, what it's waiting on, or how far behind it is.

## Who uses it
NUCO's own team in internal Pulse. Founders, admins, agents, PMs and developers draw the maps. Everyone else, such as content and social assistants, works from the maps and from projects launched off templates.

Today, people open a project and read a pile of task cards. The page shows a percentage and a risk score, but not the shape of the work. Example: "Ask the Experts" reads "25% behind, risk 90," and you can't see that the whole project is stuck on one DNS step. Some projects, like Harbor Dental's website build, have no tasks at all, so there is nothing to see.

## Core insight
The plan and the progress are the same picture: you draw the route to plan a project, and you watch the work move along it to manage it.

## Why this, why now
Projects are being missed now. Dave's words: "We need to fix this or we will continue to 'trip' over what is not getting done when it needs to." The map view is wanted tomorrow, Tue Oct 6, if the build is straightforward, and Friday, Oct 9, 2026 at the latest, with the builder following.

## What success looks like
By Friday, Oct 16, 2026, two things are true. Every active project can be read on its map at a glance to see whether it's on track, without opening the task board. And every new project started that week is built on the map, not as a task list.

## What this is not
It is not a map across all projects at once, which was set aside as too big. It is not for retail Pulse; if retail ever gets it, it goes through its own gate. It does not replace the task board, which stays one click away.
